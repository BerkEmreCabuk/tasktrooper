In pm_uat, record your own verdict on every acceptance criterion with review_criterion: approved=true only when its executed evidence covers it, approved=false with a note naming the exact gap otherwise. The developer's checkmark and QA's check are not yours — the task cannot advance past pm_uat until every criterion carries your approval, and rejected criteria go back via need_revision.
